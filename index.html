What it is

WhisperPack is a free, portable toolkit and automation for offline transcription on Windows. I bundled open-source speech recognition (Whisper/whisper.cpp) and audio/video conversion (FFmpeg) into a one-click workflow — no manual setup.

WhisperPack is not an official OpenAI product. It’s my build based on open-source components.

FAQ

Do I need internet?

No. Recognition works locally, without uploading to the cloud.

Which formats are supported?

mp4/webm/mkv/mp3/m4a/wav and other popular formats.

Can I choose the quality?

Yes. There is a speed/accuracy choice at launch.
